Context Risks & Failure Modes

What goes wrong when context is mismanaged — and why it matters.

Context Poisoning

Inaccurate or irrelevant data enters Messages and causes reasoning drift and recurring tool errors.

Lossy Condensation

When the context limit is hit, Bob summarizes older segments — dropping critical nuance permanently.

Transcript Bloat

Large single dumps consume token headroom rapidly, leaving less space for reasoning.
