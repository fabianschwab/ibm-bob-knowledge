What you can do with Bob

Track usage and optimize

Bobalytics provides analytics and insights into how Bob contributes to your software development process. Use it to understand Bob's impact on your codebase, track Bobcoin spending, and monitor team adoption across your organization.

Three key performance indicators

  • Adoption rate: Average daily active users as a percentage of total licensed seats.
  • Bob factor: The percentage of committed lines of code that Bob created.
  • Bobcoin spend: Total Bobcoins consumed in the selected period.

Assess efficiency

Use Bob factor and Bobcoin spend together. A high Bob factor with low spend indicates Bob is being used effectively.

Bobalytics is available exclusively for Enterprise plan users, in the Admin area of the IBM Bob web portal. Source: Bobalytics
