Key features

Bob tips

Bob tips continuously analyzes the files open in your editor and flags code quality issues at the function level. It runs in the background with no manual scan or configuration.

What it detects

  • High cyclomatic complexity: A value of 10 or higher, caused by deep nesting, many branches, or large switch statements.
  • Low maintainability: A maintainability index of 70 or lower, often caused by long functions with too many responsibilities.

How you see it

  • The function gets a purple underline in your editor.
  • Hover over the underline to see the issue and AI-powered refactoring suggestions.
  • All findings are collected in the Bob Findings panel across your open files.

Act on a finding

  • Click Fix with Bob in the tooltip to open a chat with the issue as context.
  • Use @problems in chat to reference findings.
  • Turn the feature on or off in Bob Settings → Bob findings. Disabling it removes existing findings.
