Key features

Bobalytics

Bobalytics shows how Bob contributes to your software development: its impact on your codebase, Bobcoin spending, and team adoption. It is available only on the Enterprise plan.

Open it

  • Go to bob.ibm.com and log in.
  • Click Admin, then select the Bobalytics tab.
  • The four sections are Metrics, Insights, Today, and My board.

The three key metrics

  • Adoption rate: Average daily active users as a percentage of licensed seats.
  • Bob factor: The percentage of committed lines of code that Bob created.
  • Bobcoin spend: The Bobcoins consumed in the selected period, also shown as a share of your budget.

Use it well

  • Read Bob factor and Bobcoin spend together to judge efficiency. High spend with a low Bob factor can mean Bob is used for research or exploration rather than committed code.
  • Filter by time range or team, and switch between organization, team, and user scope.
  • Check it weekly or monthly to spot trends early.
  • Select Export to download tables as CSV files in a ZIP archive.

Privacy

  • Users in team-level charts are anonymized as User 1, User 2, and so on.
  • Admins can only see their own individual data.
